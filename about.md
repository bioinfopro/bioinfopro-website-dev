# About Our Mission

We focus on delivering high-impact, low-overhead technical solutions. 

## Our Core Technologies

* **GitHub Pages & Jekyll:** Lightning-fast, static generation with zero database overhead.
* **Markdown Driven:** Content writers don't need to know HTML. They just write `.md` files.
* **Component Architecture:** Reusable liquid includes keep our layouts DRY (Don't Repeat Yourself).

### Typography Rules in Action

1. **Space Grotesk** is used for all Headings (H1 to H6).
2. **Inter** is used for paragraph and body text for high readability.
