# About us

The Association of Bioinformatics Professionals creates skill-based standards for the professional practice of bioinformatics. We close the gap between employers and bioinformatics professional by shifting the focus from academic credentials to practical skills. The ABP is both an organization and a community made to empower the people who can do bioinformatics, no matter the path they followed. 

## Why we exist

Bioinformatics grew out of academic labs incredibly fast, without anyone being able to pause to define the job. There's still no formal definition of what a bioinformatician's job is. and there's no professional body to set one; so when employers need to judge skills, most have to fall back on academic credentials instead. When over 80% of the computational biology workforce is self-taught or trained outside a formal doctoral degree program, academic filters leave out highly capable individuals, ceating the appearance of a talent shortage. 

That gap is why we exist. To define bioinformatics as an skills-based profession with multiple specializations.
